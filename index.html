Experience

Hands-on medtech research & strategy.

Before launching this service, I spent nearly a year as Business Development Associate at SageSpectra, an early-stage biomedical device startup seeking seed-stage funding while running animal testing on its device. There I built every deliverable solo and from scratch: market reports, TAM / SAM / SOM market sizing, 5-year financial forecasts, competitive analyses, and competitor matrices, plus target lists of angel investors and venture-capital firms.

I worked the same way I'll work for you. I started from the client's specific goals, combined the data they provided with rigorous independent research from legitimate sources, and delivered finished, decision-ready documents on a schedule, both quick weekly reports and longer multi-week projects.

That is exactly the work in my Market Brief, Market Report, and Strategy Package. It's the same research, modeling, and analysis, now packaged into fixed-scope, fixed-price deliverables for medtech founders.

Questions

Frequently asked.

Do you sign NDAs?
Yes. I'm glad to sign a mutual NDA before you share anything proprietary, and where an engagement warrants it I'll agree a limited non-solicitation or exclusivity term scoped to that project. Confidentiality is the default on every engagement. Your device, data, and findings stay private.
Who am I contracting with?
All engagements are with ZKK Consulting LLC, a Texas limited liability company. Zolomon Kaliser is the trade name it operates under. The Master Services Agreement, Statement of Work, mutual NDA, and every invoice are issued in the name of ZKK Consulting LLC.
How does payment work?
Each package is a fixed price split into two parts. We start with a quick intake and scoping call to confirm fit and scope. Then a 50% deposit, paid by card through a secure Stripe link, kicks off the work and starts your timeline. The remaining 50% is invoiced separately on completion, when the finished deliverable is in your hands. This applies to all three packages.
Do you offer revisions?
One round of revisions is included with every package. After the findings walkthrough, you can send changes and I'll refine the deliverable so it matches what we scoped.
How long does it take?
Timelines run from the scoping call: Market Brief in 5 business days, Market Report in 7, and the Strategy Package in 14. On a tighter deadline? Mention it when you email and I'll tell you what's possible.
What if my project doesn't fit a package?
Email me what you're trying to decide. Every engagement starts with a conversation, and scope can be tailored. If your project doesn't fit one of the three packages, we'll work through it on the scoping call, and I'll send a custom quote based on my estimated hours — the same basis the package prices are built on. The packages are a starting point, not a ceiling.
How is the research sourced?
Every assumption and figure is documented and cited, so you and your investors can trace each number back to its source. The goal is research you can defend in a board meeting, not a black box.
